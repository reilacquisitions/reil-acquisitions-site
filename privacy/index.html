Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we will never do with it.

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

If you contact us or download our checklist, we use your details to respond to you and to send you the material you asked for. We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to lists you did not ask for.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us — name, email address, phone number, company, and anything you write in a message or inquiry form.
  • Form submissions — routed through FormSubmit and delivered to our business inbox.
  • Basic technical data — our hosting provider records standard request logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and provide the resources you requested.
  • To evaluate whether we are a fit for your acquisition or exit goals.
  • To keep records of our communications and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

Confidentiality of deal information

Business and financial information shared during an engagement is treated as strictly confidential. We market businesses on a blind basis, and we will sign a mutual NDA before you share sensitive financial detail. Information about your company is disclosed to a counterparty only after you approve it.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate the site and our communications (such as our email and form providers), or where we are required to by law.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time.
  • You can unsubscribe from any email we send you using the link included in it.
  • You may opt out of our list entirely by emailing us.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to remove your information: hello@reilacquisitions.com, or use the form on our contact page.
