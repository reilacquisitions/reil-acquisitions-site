About REIL

Operators first. Advisors second.

REIL (Retire Early In Life) Acquisitions is a Midwest M&A advisory built by people who have actually bought, run, and sold companies — not by brokers who have only watched other people do it.

Why we exist

Most mid-market deals fail for reasons that have nothing to do with price: undisclosed liabilities, owner dependency, customer concentration, and sellers who were never prepared to be sold. By the time those problems surface, everyone has spent real money and real months.

We built REIL to surface those issues early and to represent both sides honestly. Buyers get vetted, capital-ready opportunities instead of a listings feed. Sellers get a confidential process that protects their business, their employees, and their legacy until a serious buyer is under NDA.

Our focus is deliberately narrow: $5M–$100M revenue businesses across Wisconsin, Illinois, Iowa, Michigan, and Minnesota — the markets we know, the lenders we work with, and the owners we can genuinely help.
